What this progress means

One normalized 768-number vector is stored for each English-labelled work with a non-empty abstract. Long inputs are cut at the model’s 512-token limit; the full content table stays unchanged. This is the embedding backfill, not the later search-index build.

The worker never stops your other GPU services. It waits for free memory, and temporary failures are retried. A deliberate pause stays paused after reboot. Source files and the model version are pinned; validation failures require attention rather than silently skipping records.
